English

Scam Dojo collects no personal information. It has no account, no ads and no tracking. Everything the app saves stays on your device.

1. Who makes this app

Scam Dojo is a practice app made by Kritika Sharma, an independent developer in India. For any question about this policy, email sharmakritu617@gmail.com.

2. What the app saves, on your device only

  • The language you chose (Hindi or English).
  • Whether sound is on or off.
  • Your best stars for each practice call. Your level and badges are worked out from these.

This is kept in the app's private storage on your device and is never sent anywhere. To delete it, open Help → Reset all progress, or uninstall the app.

3. What the app does not collect

The app does not collect your name, phone number, contacts, messages, call history, location, photos, microphone recordings, device identifiers or advertising ID. There is no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

4. Voice, ringtone and vibration

The practice caller's lines are read aloud by your device's own text-to-speech engine. Only the practice script written in the app is passed to that engine, never information about you. The engine is part of your phone and follows its maker's privacy policy. The ringtone and vibration are produced on your device.

5. Links to outside services

Some buttons open your phone's dialler for the national cyber fraud helpline 1930, or the websites cybercrime.gov.in and sancharsaathi.gov.in. Once you leave the app, those services' own policies apply.

Scam Dojo is not from, or connected with, any bank, police force or government body. All names and phone numbers in the practice calls are made up.

6. Children

The app is made for adults and families. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Security

Because no personal information leaves your device, there is nothing held by us that could be lost or shared.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version ever needs to collect any information, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the effective date above will change.

9. Contact
